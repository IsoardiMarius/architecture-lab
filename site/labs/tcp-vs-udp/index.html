Architecture note: The two timelines are conceptual, not synchronized packet captures. TCP sequence numbers, cumulative ACKs, windowing, and congestion control are simplified. TCP can retransmit unacknowledged data; it cannot guarantee delivery if a connection permanently fails. UDP itself does not detect or recover losses, but applications or protocols such as QUIC can implement these functions above UDP. Neither transport protocol provides encryption on its own.
